# 0000. Template

- **Status:** template
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Project:** PN

## Context
What forced a decision. Real constraints only: time, hardware, cost, existing skills.

## Options considered
For each: what it is, why it was plausible, its concrete cost or drawback here.
At least two genuine alternatives — an ADR with one option is a rationalization.

## Decision
What was chosen, and the deciding factor.

## Consequences
What this makes easy. What it makes hard. What would have to change to revisit it.

## Evidence
Benchmarks, eval scores, cost figures. If decided on judgment rather than data, say so.
